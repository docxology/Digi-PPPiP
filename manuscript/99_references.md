# References {#sec:references}

Bibliography lives in [`manuscript/references.bib`](references.bib) and is resolved by Pandoc during rendering. All manuscript citations use bracketed Pandoc syntax so the renderer, not the prose, controls bibliography formatting and numbering.

Search tools and Perplexity are discovery aids only. A citekey should enter the manuscript only after the title, authors, year, venue, and DOI or stable URL have been checked against a DOI resolver, publisher page, PubMed, arXiv, Crossref, DataCite, official government page, official standards page, or archival record.
