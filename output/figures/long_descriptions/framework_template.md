# Long description: fig:framework_template

Reusable visual grammar template that distinguishes actors, artifacts, signals, contexts, diagnostics, evidence maps, caveats, non-color encodings, and long-description obligations.

Generator: `generate_framework_template`.
Claim status: conceptual.
Manuscript section: integrative.
Method-source family: research_through_design.
Placement: main.
Reading order: title and visible claim-status stamp first, then the main panel from left to right or top to bottom, then legends, callouts, and footer caveat.
Caveat: the figure is conceptual, protocol, audit, analytic-simulation, or placeholder material according to the claim status; it is not participant outcome evidence unless explicitly stated.
Evidence boundary: stronger claims require the future evidence named by the manuscript claim ledger and source-quality gates.
